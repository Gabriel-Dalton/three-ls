# Contributing

Useful contributions include clearer explanations, practical examples, translations, teaching activities, and corrections supported by sources.

Open an issue to suggest a change, or submit a pull request with a short explanation of what it improves. Small contributions are welcome.

If you try the workshop or worksheet, tell us the setting, what participants found useful, where they got stuck, and what you changed. Share anonymised feedback with permission. We will distinguish reports of use from evidence of environmental outcomes.

## Keep the framework clear

Keep the three steps recognisable: Learn, Lighten, Lead. Explain adaptations and preserve attribution. Prefer plain language and activities that work without specialist tools or paid accounts.

## Support your claims

For environmental figures, include the original source, date, method, scope, and limitations. Label examples as fictional unless they describe documented work you have permission to share. Follow the distinctions in [evidence and measurement](EVIDENCE.md).

## Respect people and their information

Keep discussions constructive and respectful. Share only material you have permission to publish. Exclude private messages, personal contact details, client information, and internal records. Use synthetic details for illustrative examples.

Use descriptive links, semantic headings, and text alternatives for meaningful visuals. Check that changes preserve accessibility and that relative links resolve.
