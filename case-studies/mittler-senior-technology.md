# A lighter website for Mittler Senior Technology

Mittler Senior Technology teaches older adults to use computers and smartphones. Oasis of Change redesigned its website. This is a real project with a [published case study](https://oasisofchange.com/case-studies/mittler-senior-technology/), reviewed here on 8 October 2026.

The three steps below are a retrospective reading of that work. They do not establish that the project was a formal trial of the framework.

## Learn: look beyond the hosting label

The site was already using hosting described as green. The review still found large images, unnecessary code, and heavy pages. Choosing a host had not resolved what the website asked visitors to load.

## Lighten: change the website

The redesign addressed page structure, front-end efficiency, and unnecessary weight, alongside accessibility improvements. The public case study reports these before-and-after figures from Website Carbon Calculator:

| Per-visit estimate | Before | After | Reported reduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Energy | 0.0139 kWh | 0.00025 kWh | 98.2% |
| Carbon | 5.31 g | 0.10 g | 98.1% |

These are modelled energy and carbon estimates, not electricity readings from the servers.

## Lead: make the result available to others

Oasis published the comparison. Here, that publication serves the Lead step: another team can examine the work and ask similar questions about its own site. We do not have evidence here of how many teams subsequently acted on it.

## What this example can establish

The figures describe this before-and-after comparison. They do not promise the same saving for another redesign, establish annual savings from actual traffic, or isolate the effect of each technical change.

This repository does not include the original audit exports or a complete reproduction record. Treat the table as a reported project result with that limitation. A stronger future record would include dated page-level tests, tool versions, settings, and model inputs.

When applying the 3 Ls to your own project, record those details from the start. The [worksheet](../templates/action-plan.md) gives you a place to do that.
