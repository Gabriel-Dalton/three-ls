## Learn

- What need does this technology meet?
- What did I learn about its physical footprint?
- What is my source, its date, and its scope?
- Is the information observed, measured, modelled, or unknown?
- What question remains unanswered?

## Lighten

- What specific change will I try?
- Why is this change useful?
- What is the starting point, and how will I record it?
- How will I preserve accessibility, privacy, security, and usefulness?
- How will I compare the result under similar conditions?

## Lead

- Who will I involve or share this with?
- What evidence can I share responsibly?
- What next action will I propose?
- Who can change the relevant default, policy, or system?

## Review

- What happened?
- Did the technology still meet the need?
- What did I observe or measure? What remains uncertain?
- Will I keep, revise, or stop the change?
- What will I learn or try next?
