# The 3 Ls

## Learn. Lighten. Lead.

A practical framework for digital sustainability, created by [Gabriel Dalton](https://gabrieldalton.com).

The internet has a physical footprint: devices, networks, servers, electricity, cooling, and the materials used to build them. Digital sustainability belongs in the way we learn about technology, use it, and build it.

The 3 Ls turn that awareness into action:

| Step | What it means | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Learn** | Understand the environmental cost of the technology you use. | Pick one tool. Find out what its provider discloses, and what remains unknown. |
| **Lighten** | Make intentional choices to reduce unnecessary digital demand. | Change one habit, setting, or design decision while keeping the service useful and accessible. |
| **Lead** | Share what you learn and help change the systems around you. | Bring one question or practical improvement to your classroom, workplace, or community. |

You can start with a single decision. You can also use the framework to guide a lesson, a team discussion, or a digital project. Repeat it as your understanding grows.

### Try it this week

1. **Learn:** choose a digital activity you repeat often. What does it need to accomplish? What resources does it use? What evidence can you find?
2. **Lighten:** try one change. Remove an unnecessary download, use an appropriate video resolution, or choose a simpler tool when it meets your needs.
3. **Lead:** explain the change to one person. Share what you observed, what you could measure, and what you still do not know.

Use the [action worksheet](templates/action-plan.md) to record your decision and review it after a week.

### Where it comes from

I introduced the 3 Ls in my TEDx talk, [*The Missing Piece of AI & Tech Literacy*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9L3bjj3GkSI), at TEDxEcole Mission Secondary in February 2026, and returned to the framework in *Digital Sustainability in the Age of AI*, my presentation at the Consulate General of Ireland in Vancouver on 1 October 2026.

This repository develops the framework into reusable guides and activities. The examples and workshop are practical extensions of the three steps. They are not a transcript of either presentation.
