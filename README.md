# The 3 Ls: Learn, Lighten, Lead

By [Gabriel Dalton](https://gabrieldalton.com).

I developed the 3 Ls through my work on digital sustainability at [Oasis of Change](https://oasisofchange.com/). I wanted people to leave a conversation about the environmental cost of technology knowing what they could do next.

**Learn what your technology costs. Lighten what you can. Lead by bringing others into the work.**

You do not need to be a developer to use it. Teachers influence what students learn, buyers influence what suppliers disclose, and developers influence what every visitor downloads. Start with the decision you can change.

| Step | What it means | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Learn** | Understand the environmental cost of the technology you use. | Pick one tool. Find out what its provider discloses, and what remains unknown. |
| **Lighten** | Make intentional choices to reduce unnecessary digital demand. | Change one habit, setting, or design decision while keeping the service useful and accessible. |
| **Lead** | Share what you learn and help change the systems around you. | Bring one question or practical improvement to your classroom, workplace, or community. |

## Why these three words?

Building websites taught me to think about what happens underneath the page. My sustainability work made me ask who pays for the electricity, cooling, and hardware behind it. The 3 Ls connect understanding that footprint to changing something and sharing what you learn.

I introduced the framework in my [TEDx talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9L3bjj3GkSI) in February 2026 and returned to it at the Consulate General of Ireland in Vancouver on 1 October 2026. Read [how it developed](ORIGIN.md).

## Start with a real project

[Mittler Senior Technology](case-studies/mittler-senior-technology.md) is a website redesign from our work at Oasis of Change. Its hosting was already described as green, yet the pages still had unnecessary weight. The case study walks through what we learned, what changed, and how publishing the results carries the work further.

The energy and carbon results are modelled estimates. The case study explains their limits alongside the improvement.

## Use it yourself

Read [the framework](FRAMEWORK.md), choose one activity, and try one change for a week. Record what happened and share something another person can use.

- [Action worksheet](templates/action-plan.md)
- [30-minute workshop](workshops/30-minute-workshop.md)
- [Illustrative examples](examples/README.md)

The worksheet and workshop are initial resources. This repository does not yet document a completed pilot or participant feedback. If you try them, [tell us what worked and what needed changing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

For environmental claims, use the [evidence guide](EVIDENCE.md). The framework helps organise action; it does not calculate a footprint or certify a product.
