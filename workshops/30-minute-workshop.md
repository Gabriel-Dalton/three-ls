# A 30-minute 3 Ls workshop

**Purpose:** each participant leaves with one practical digital sustainability action and a way to review it.

**Audience:** a classroom, workplace, or community group. No technical background is required.

**Materials:** the [framework](../FRAMEWORK.md), [examples](../examples/README.md), and an [action worksheet](../templates/action-plan.md) per person or group. Printed copies work. No account or personal device is required.

## 0–5 minutes: introduce

Explain that online services depend on physical infrastructure. Introduce Learn, Lighten, Lead using the table in the [README](../README.md).

Ask participants to name a digital activity they use regularly. They can choose a fictional scenario instead of sharing personal habits.

## 5–12 minutes: Learn

In pairs or individually, choose one activity and discuss:

- What does it help you do?
- What physical resources might it depend on?
- What do you know, and what would you need to find out?

Use a supplied example or source if internet access is unavailable. Encourage people to write down unknowns. The aim is to ask useful questions, not invent a footprint figure.

## 12–20 minutes: Lighten

Choose one change to try. Make it specific and realistic within a week.

Ask: will it still meet the need? Could it create a barrier for someone else? How could you tell whether it helped?

Record the change and the review method on the worksheet.

## 20–27 minutes: Lead

Each person or group chooses someone to involve and a next action. This might be sharing a setting, proposing a team practice, or asking a vendor for information.

Invite optional short reports: one thing learned, one change, one question. Accept spoken, written, or other accessible ways of contributing.

## 27–30 minutes: close

Set a review date. Remind participants to report observations honestly and distinguish them from environmental estimates.

At the review, discuss what worked, what introduced difficulties, and what the next cycle of Learn, Lighten, Lead should address.
